# Privacy Policy
Shorts Agent is a personal application used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
- The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, with the permissions granted by the owner (video upload and channel management).
- The app does not collect, sell, share or disclose personal data of any other person.
- Access tokens are stored only as private secrets in the owner's own GitHub account.
- No data is transferred to third parties, apart from the Google/YouTube APIs needed to publish the videos.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
